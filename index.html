Route Planner • No Google Required

Pick a start, pick an end, compare routes, then show the selected route on the map.

This page reads your location CSV, removes duplicate exact entries, lets you filter by type, ranks route choices, shows mileage and drive time, adds a weather delay note, and draws the chosen route on the map.

How to use it
Start typing in each location box and choose an exact match from the dropdown list. Then press Find Routes. Click any route bar below to push that route onto the map.
